Recipe Instructions

2-koshary

Koshary is a famous Egyptian street food made with layers of rice, pasta, and lentils, topped with a rich tomato sauce, crispy fried onions, and chickpeas. It is a hearty and flavorful dish loved across Egypt.

Ingredients

pasta/rice

  • Chickpeas rinsed and drained
  • Ditalini pasta

Fried Onions

  • Onions, sliced thin into rings
  • Salt
  • Cornstarch
  • Vegetable oil

Tomato Sauce

  • Fried onions
  • Reserved vegetable oil
  • Garlic cloves
  • Tomato paste
  • Tomato sauce
  • Water
  • Salt and pepper
  • White vinegar
  • Paprika

Lentil Rice

  • Brown lentils, rinsed and drained
  • Rice, rinsed and drained
  • Vermicelli, toasted
  • Water
  • Hot water
  • Ground cumin

Dukka / Cumin Sauce

  • White vinegar
  • Garlic, crushed
  • Hot water
  • Ground cumin
  • Salt, to taste
  • Ground black pepper
  • Paprika

Cooking Instructions

  1. Cook the rice in salted water until fully done.
  2. Boil the lentils in water until tender, then drain them.
  3. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water according to the package instructions.
  4. In a pan, cook chopped onions in oil until they become crispy and golden brown.
  5. In another pan, prepare the tomato sauce by cooking garlic in oil.
  6. Add tomato sauce, salt, pepper, and a little vinegar, then simmer for 10 minutes.
  7. Warm the chickpeas in a small pot with a little water.
  8. To serve, place a layer of rice in a bowl, then add lentils and pasta.
  9. Top with tomato sauce, chickpeas, and crispy fried onions.
  10. Serve hot and add extra vinegar or hot sauce if desired.
