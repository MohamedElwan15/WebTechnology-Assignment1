Molokhia

Molokhia is a traditional Egyptian dish made from finely chopped jute leaves, cooked with garlic and coriander, and served over rice or with bread. It is a flavorful, healthy, and comforting meal loved throughout Egypt.

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh or frozen molokhia leaves, finely chopped
  • 1 whole chicken or 500 g chicken pieces
  • 6 cups water or chicken broth
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp salt (or to taste)
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil or butter
  • 1 cup cooked white rice (for serving)

Cooking Instructions

  1. In a large pot, boil the chicken in water or chicken broth until fully cooked.
  2. Remove the chicken, shred it, and set aside. Keep the broth in the pot.
  3. Add the molokhia leaves to the hot broth and cook on medium heat for 5–7 minutes (do not overcook).
  4. In a small pan, heat oil or butter and sauté garlic and coriander until fragrant.
  5. Pour the garlic-coriander mixture into the molokhia pot and stir well.
  6. Add salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Return the shredded chicken to the pot if desired.
  8. Serve hot over cooked rice or with warm Egyptian bread.

Enjoy your delicious Molokhia!
