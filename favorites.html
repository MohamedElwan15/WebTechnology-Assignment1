My Favorite Recipes

Macaroni Bashamel

Macaroni Bashamel is a classic Egyptian dish that combines pasta with a creamy bachamel sauce. It is a comforting and flavorful meal that is often enjoyed as a main course.

Main Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Flour
  • Mozzarella Cheese
  • Minced meat

Chicken-ranch pizza

Chicken-ranch pizza is a delicious combination of crispy pizza crust, creamy ranch dressing, tender chicken pieces, and melted mozzarella cheese. It is a popular choice for pizza lovers who enjoy a savory and tangy flavor profile.

Main Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Tomato sauce
  • Chicken
  • Ranch sauce

Koshari

Koshari is a popular Egyptian street food made with a combination of rice, lentils, pasta, and topped with a spicy tomato sauce and crispy fried onions. It is a hearty and flavorful dish that is loved by many.

Main Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Rice
  • Onions
  • Tomato sauce
  • Lentils
  • Daka

Molokhia

Molokhia is a traditional Middle Eastern dish made from the leaves of the molokhia plant and marinated with garlic and coriander. It is usually served with rice and chicken or beef.

Main Ingredients

  • Molokhia plant
  • Garlic
  • Coriander
  • Chicken broth
